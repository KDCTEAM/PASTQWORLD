Apply Now for the 2024 Nigeria NELFUND Student Loan

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND) has launched a new student loan program to make higher education more accessible.

```

Eligibility:

  • Enrolled in a public tertiary institution (university, polytechnic, college of education, or vocational school)
  • No income or parental loan history restrictions

Repayment:

  • Starts two years after NYSC service
  • 10% of salary or income deducted through payroll system
  • Extension possible in case of hardship
  • Loan forgiveness in cases of permanent disability or death

Important Notes:

  • False information or manipulation can lead to jail term or fine
  • Online application process is free and transparent

How to Apply:

Visit the NELFUND application portal (opened May 24th, 2024)

  • Required documents:
  • National Identity Number (NIN)
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
  • Student matriculation or registration number
  • JAMB admission letter
```
